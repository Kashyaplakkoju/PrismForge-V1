Canonical face topology from google-ai-edge/mediapipe commit
8317ba78778738ba90a521e7e4580a2ba0129c81, under Apache-2.0.
https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe/blob/8317ba78778738ba90a521e7e4580a2ba0129c81/mediapipe/modules/face_geometry/data/canonical_face_model.obj

The app computes approximate facial normals and relative-depth self-occlusion
from these landmarks locally. This is not Google's proprietary Meet renderer
and does not reconstruct scene lighting or remove shadows baked into the input.
No personal test images are distributed with these model assets.
